Migration is one-way

You receive AKT on Solana, not SOL. Successfully migrated AKT cannot be moved back through this portal. AKT on Solana cannot currently be staked. Do not migrate funds you need for staking or use on Akash.

Check your wallet and destination

You authorize the transaction in your own wallet. The portal does not need your private key or seed phrase. Check the network, amount, fees and full destination address before signing. A valid address does not prove you control it or that an exchange supports AKT deposits on Solana. Mistakes or compromised keys can permanently lose your funds.
