AKT on Solana may not support all uses of AKT on the Akash blockchain, including staking. Only unbonded AKT can be migrated. If your AKT is bonded (staked), you must unbond it first and can migrate it only after the Akash blockchain's unbonding period (currently 21 days) ends.
